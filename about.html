About Maker Jogos

Handmade board games, tested at the table and delivered for you to print.

Who makes the games

I'm André Melhor Miranda. I have a degree in Mathematics, a master's in Computer Science from the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA, Brazil), and I teach Mathematics and Science. I'm also a musician and, for years, I have been designing board games.

Among the games I have created are Brasilidades, Pense Rápido and Congresso em Chamas. I also developed a Game Design course, because making a game is a craft you can learn.

Why Print & Play

Physical board games are expensive to produce and ship. Print & Play solves that: you buy the file, print it and play the same day. For people who like to make things, building your own game is part of the fun.

From paper to table

I'm the kind of person who solves things with my own hands: woodworking, glue, a craft knife and plenty of prototypes. Every Maker Jogos game goes through that process of building, playing, failing and adjusting before it reaches you.
