Before you write

A few quick answers:

I paid but did not receive the file.

Check your Spam and Promotions folders and make sure the e-mail you entered at checkout is correct. Bank slips are only released after the bank confirms the payment. If you still have no access, write to us with the e-mail used for the purchase and the name of the game.

How do I get a refund?

See Refunds, payment and access problems on the How it works page.

Does a box with the game ship to me?

No. It is a Print & Play game: you receive the file and print it at home or at a print shop. See how it works.
