How it works

Buy, print and play. See each step below.

What is Print & Play

Print & Play (PnP) is a game you receive as a digital file. Instead of a box arriving in the mail, you print the pieces at home or at a print shop, cut them out and assemble them. You can start playing the same day you buy.

Important: what you buy is the file. No physical item is shipped.

Step 1: how to buy

  1. Pick a game and click Buy. The Kiwify payment page opens in a new tab. Your payment details never go through this site.
  2. Fill in your details with an e-mail you can access: that is where your access to the file will be sent.
  3. Choose a payment method and confirm.
  4. Once the payment is approved, access to the file arrives at the e-mail used for the purchase.
Refunds, payment and access problems

Refunds: purchases made in Brazil can be refunded within 7 calendar days, with no need to give a reason (right of withdrawal under the Brazilian Consumer Code), through Kiwify's "My orders" page. For purchases from other countries, please contact us.

Payment: the payment methods available depend on the game and on your country. You will see the options on the checkout page. With some methods, such as bank slips, access is only released after the payment is confirmed, which can take a few days.

Did not receive the file? Check your Spam and Promotions folders and make sure the e-mail you entered at checkout is correct. If you still have no access, get in touch with the e-mail used for the purchase and the name of the game.

Step 2: how to print

  1. Open the PDF and check the paper size in the file (usually A4).
  2. In the print dialog, use 100% scale (actual size). Avoid "fit to page", which changes the size of the pieces and ruins cutting and fitting.
  3. Print in color and at good quality.
  4. Prefer heavier paper for cards and board. If you only have regular paper, glue the sheets to thin cardboard or card stock.
  5. No printer at home? Take the PDF to a print shop and ask for a color print at actual size.

Step 3: how to build and play

  1. Cut out the pieces along the cutting lines. A ruler and a craft knife (with a cutting mat) give a better finish than scissors.
  2. For extra durability, glue the front and back of cards and tokens before cutting.
  3. Sort the components following the rulebook.
  4. Read the rules once before setting up the table, then start playing.
